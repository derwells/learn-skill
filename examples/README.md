# Examples

Two stretches of real sessions from my Bayesian statistics course, lightly trimmed. Both are from the terminal. Figures were drawn by the tutor mid-session and pushed to my phone over Telegram; they are re-rendered here from the tutor's code. My messages are as typed, some of them voice-dictated.

- [A prior that looked harmless](a-prior-that-looked-harmless.md). A war story about a prior that was quietly telling the model an effect didn't exist, and the exchange where the "weak vs strong" confusion got sorted out.
- [Where the interval comes from](where-the-interval-comes-from.md). The first regression lesson. A fitted model as a bag of lines, and what you do with it when someone asks for a number.

What the notebook looks like after a session is in the [main README](../README.md#how-it-teaches).
